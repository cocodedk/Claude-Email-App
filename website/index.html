Features

What the app does.

Connect your mailbox, send commands, follow the replies and get notified.

01 · Your mailbox

Works with your own mailbox.

Mail is received over IMAP and sent over SMTP, the standard ways a mail app talks to a mail provider. Commands and replies travel as email in the claude-email message format.

02 · Sign-in details

Sign-in details stored encrypted.

Your mail password and shared secret are stored encrypted, with a key kept in the Android Keystore. The password goes only to your own mail server, to sign in.

03 · Command & reply

Commands and replies in conversations.

Each conversation shows its title, the sender, a preview and a label for the latest message, such as “needs reply” or “result”.

04 · Shared secret

A shared secret for your service.

Commands carry the shared secret you set in the app. Your claude-email service checks it before it accepts a command.

05 · Notifications

Hear back when the app is closed.

A notification arrives when a new message comes in and the app is not open on screen. They are on by default. To stop all alerts at once, turn off this app’s notifications in Android settings.

06 · Dark theme

Easy on the eyes.

Read conversations in a dark interface, and expand long replies when you want the rest.

Install

Three steps to your first command.

You need an existing email account and a running claude-email service. The app has no account of its own.

  1. 01 / Download

    Download the signed APK.

    Get the latest release straight from GitHub. An APK is Android’s installation file.

  2. 02 / Install

    Install on Android 7.0 or newer.

    Open the downloaded file. On Android 8 or newer, allow installs from the app you opened it with. On Android 7, turn on “Unknown sources”. Then confirm the installation.

  3. 03 / Set up

    Add your details, then send a command.

    Enter your mail account and the details of its mail servers, then tap “Test and save”. Next tap “New command”, enter the project path and your command, and tap “Send”.
